Zhen (Colin) Li

Hello, I am Zhen(Colin) Li , an MSc thesis student at the GrUVi Lab of SFU, under the supervision of Prof. Andrea Tagliasacchi. I obtained my bachelor's degree of the dual degree program between Zhejiang University and Simon Fraser University, majoring in computer science.

My research interests lie in 3D computer vision and computer graphics. My current research focuses on robot-based material capture, neural BRDF modeling, and video models for material appearance. Before this, I worked on large-scale scene reconstruction.

RoboCloth: A Large-Scale Real Cloth Material Dataset for Neural Reflectance Reconstruction
Zhen (Colin) Li*, Haoran Qin*, Francesco Di Sario, Daniel Rebain, Andrea Tagliasacchi
* equal contribution
Paper / Project Page / Code / Dataset
